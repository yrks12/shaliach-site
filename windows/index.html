Shaliach / Windows

Shaliach for Windows

Your own team of Claude Code agents on your PC. Our team installs it and does the first setup with you. You need Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit, and a Claude account. Some PCs need one restart.

Book a demo

Shaliach for Business: a paid product, sold to businesses only. We install it on your Mac or server and do the first setup with you.

Three steps

  1. Book a demo by email: support@shaliach.me.
  2. We install it. Our team sets up Shaliach on your PC. If it needs a restart, save anything open first: it opens again by itself and carries on.
  3. Sign in to Claude. You sign in with your own Claude account. Then your team is ready.

What it installs

Shaliach runs your team inside Windows' own Linux layer (WSL2). Setup turns WSL2 on if it's off, adds a separate Linux workspace named Shaliach, and installs the Shaliach server there, reachable only from this PC. Anything you already have in WSL is left alone. Your agents use your own Claude account; nothing goes to us. See Privacy.

If setup stops with a problem, it says what to do in plain words. For help, write to support@shaliach.me.
