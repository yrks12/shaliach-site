Support

Write to admin@yairtech.co.uk. We aim to answer within 3 working days. Tell us your iPhone or Mac model, the app version and what you saw. Never send your access token or pairing code.

Getting started

  1. Shaliach is the iPhone and Mac window for a Shaliach server that you run, on your own Mac or a small Linux server. The apps don't include a server: our team installs one for you. Book a demo.
  2. No server yet? On the iPhone, tap Try a demo on the connect screen. It opens the whole app on sample desks, on your phone only, and sends nothing anywhere.
  3. To connect your own: on your server, open Settings, then Pair a device, and paste the pairing code into the app. Or enter the server's address and token by hand. The token is kept in your device's Keychain.

Common questions

  • "Your deck didn't answer" or "Is your VPN on?" The phone must reach your server: the same network, or the VPN you use for it. Check that the server is running and that the address is the one in your Mac app's settings.
  • The pairing code doesn't work. Codes are single-use and expire. Make a new one on your server and paste the whole code.
  • Calls don't start. Live calls need an OpenAI key on your server. Without one, everything else works.
  • Where does my data go? To your server, and from there to the AI services you set up. Nothing comes to us. Read the privacy policy.
  • Removing a device. In the app, Disconnect forgets the server and its token. On your server you can revoke the device's token at any time.
